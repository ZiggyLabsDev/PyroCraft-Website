Pyrocraft. A small survival Minecraft server. But what makes it special? What sets it apart from other Minecraft servers?

To be honest, PyroCraft isn't "special." Not special in the way most would call special, and certainly not in ways you can see. It's special in a different way.

Many could look at PyroCraft and say, "Oh, custom terrain great for building and exploring? Rewards that you get for doing certain things and participating in certain events? A small player base? Yeah, sounds like the typical small Minecraft server."

But, none of those features is what makes PyroCraft special. What sets it apart from other servers is the goal the owner, Kevo, had in mind while creating PyroCraft:

"Create a friendly, non-toxic place for people to make friends, without competition."

Kevo wanted to make a server where the goal wasn't to be the richest, be the biggest, be the strongest, or anything like that. All he wanted was a place for people from all around the world to come and play together, as friends but strangers. A place without competition. A place where it isn't about winning or losing. Kevo wanted a place where you can just have fun and make friends.

And that place is PyroCraft.
