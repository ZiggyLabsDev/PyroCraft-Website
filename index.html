PYROCRAFT• SERVER GUIDE

Commands & server info

Some basic commands to help you get around

01

Getting around

Move quickly and find your way home.

/spawnReturn to spawn.
/home <name>Teleport to your home.
/sethome <name>Set your home at your current location.
/backReturn to your previous location.
/rtpTeleport to a random location.
02

Players

Connect and communicate with the community.

/tpa <player>Request to teleport to a player.
/tpahere <player>Request a player to teleport to your location
/tpacceptAccept an incoming teleport request.
/msg <player> <message>Send a private message.
/rulesRead the server rules.
03

World & claims

Protect builds and manage your space.

/lands create <name>Start or manage a land claim.
/lands trust <player>Give a player access to your claim.
/lands untrust <player>Remove a player's access to your land claims.
/lands mapOpens a menu to veiw and buy your land claims
/lands spawn <name>Teleport to your land.
