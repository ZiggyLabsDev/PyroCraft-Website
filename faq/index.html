There are multiple ways to earn money in PyroCraft. Here are a few methods:

  • Type "/jobs browse" in chat to view and join jobs.
  • Type "/vote all" in chat and follow the links to vote for our server.

Each vote earns you a Voting Key, $250 in server balance, and one experience level. Take your key to spawn to claim an in-game reward. Voting also helps new players discover PyroCraft on public server lists.

Join the server and type /vote all in chat, then follow the voting links. Enter your exact Minecraft username on each site, stay logged into the server, and leave at least one open inventory slot for your rewards.

Take your Voting Key to spawn and use it there to claim an in-game reward.

Double-check that you entered your exact Minecraft username and wait up to two minutes. If your rewards still haven't arrived, report the issue in the PyroCraft Discord reports channel.

Each voting site has its own reset schedule. Check the note beside each link on the voting page to see when you can vote there again.
