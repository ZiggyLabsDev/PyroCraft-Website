# The new PyroCraft website is live!

We've made a new home for PyroCraft news and server information, with the essentials easier to find whether you're a regular or thinking about joining.

## Take a look around

- Check the server status, player count, and recent activity.

- Catch up on server news and announcements.
- Visit the voting page for links and reward details.

Thanks for being part of the PyroCraft community. We hope the new site makes it easier to stay up to date and find your way around.
