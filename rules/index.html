PyroCraft server rules.

Help keep PyroCraft a welcoming, fair place to build, explore, and play together.

THE COMMUNITY GUIDE

Play fair. Be kind.

These rules cover both our community spaces and the server.

01 / COMMUNITY

Respect each other

  1. Be respectful. No bullying, harassment, racism, sexism, or hate speech. Jokes are allowed, but know your limits and don't use them to genuinely hurt or target someone.
  2. No excessive toxicity. Friendly competition is fine, but don't make the server unpleasant for others.
  3. Keep chat appropriate. No NSFW, excessively offensive, or inappropriate content.
  4. No spam. Don't spam messages, mentions, emojis, or commands.
  5. No advertising. Don't advertise other servers, Discords, or services without permission.
  6. Use the correct channels. Keep messages and discussions in their appropriate channels.
02 / GAMEPLAY

Protect the world

  1. No griefing. Don't intentionally destroy or ruin another player's builds.
  2. No stealing. Respect other players' items, bases, farms, and resources.
  3. No traps. Don't create or use traps designed to intentionally kill, imprison, or permanently prevent another player from escaping.
  4. Respect claims. Don't use /sethome around someone else's claim without asking. If reported, the /home will be deleted.
  5. No cheating. Hacks, exploits, dupes, x-ray, and unfair advantages are not allowed.
  6. No scams. Don't intentionally trick players out of their items or currency.
03 / COMMUNITY CARE

Keep it welcoming

  1. Listen to staff. Follow moderator instructions and don't argue with staff decisions publicly.
  2. Have fun! Build, explore, and enjoy the server.

Consequences

Breaking the rules may result in a warning, mute, kick, or ban depending on the situation. Three warnings result in a ban.
