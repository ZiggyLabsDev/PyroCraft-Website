Imperial Charter

The Official Constitution of the Ziggy Empire

The Supreme Law and Founding Charter of the Realm

Decreed by His Majesty, King TheGreatZiggy

By royal decree, the following articles establish the laws, rights, duties, and regulations governing all citizens and visitors of the glorious Ziggy Empire.

I

Article 1 — Citizenship

The Process Citizenship is gained through a simple two-step process:
  • Sign the physical book included in your free starter kit shulker.
  • Personally hand the signed book to the great, fair, and hard-working King, TheGreatZiggy.
II

Article 2 — Housing & Property Rights

Imperial Housing Citizens have the right to occupy houses built within the Ziggy Empire, provided they keep up with their monthly payment.
Construction Restriction All housing and districts are constructed exclusively by the King. No citizen may build or structurally modify any house or building without direct Royal Approval.
External Property Citizens may own private land outside the Empire, provided it is under 30 chunks in size.
Land Registration Any land purchased outside the empire must be registered in the official database. If the Empire discovers unregistered land owned by a member, that member will be kicked from the Empire permanently.
Royal Exemption King TheGreatZiggy is entirely exempt from all Article 2 restrictions. The King holds sovereign rights to own infinite land, build anywhere at will, and is completely immune to database registration requirements.
III

Article 3 — Law & Order

Prohibited Actions Griefing, stealing, logout traps, and general trapping are strictly forbidden inside the Ziggy Empire.
Criminal Fines Any member caught violating these rules must pay a $5,000 fee to the victim and a $1,000 fee to the Empire.
The Royal Multiplier If any illegal act has been committed against the King or any member of royal status, the fine increases by 5 times:
  • $25,000 to the King
  • $5,000 to the Empire
Refusal to Pay If a citizen refuses or is unable to pay a fine within 48 hours, their property inside the Empire might be seized by the crown. Additionally, they must forfeit an Ender Pearl to the Empire (to be provided by the Empire if the citizen does not own one).
IV

Article 4 — Zoning & Infrastructure

Vertical Limits No building or modification inside the Empire may exceed a height of Y-level 120 or dig deeper than Y-level 50 without a Royal Permit.
Basements & Tunneling Citizens may dig basements directly beneath their designated plots. However, tunneling into a neighbor's plot or beneath public roads is classified as trespassing.
V

Article 5 — Foreign Policy & National Security

Border Control Non-citizens are welcome to visit the Capital but must deposit all weapons in a designated Ender Chest at the border gate.
State of War Upon a formal declaration of war by the King, all able-bodied citizens must defend the Empire or supply vital resources (food, armor, potions) to the Royal Military.
Foreign Alliances No citizen may form an independent alliance or treaty with outside factions without the express written permission of King TheGreatZiggy.
VI

Article 6 — Lèse-Majesté (Disrespect of the King)

The Law Any act of disrespect, treason, insolence, or impersonation directed toward the one true ruler, King TheGreatZiggy, is strictly forbidden within the borders of the Empire and its official communication channels.
First Offense The offender must immediately hand over one Ender Pearl to the Royal Treasury as a token of submission and pay a massive $10,000 imperial fine.
Repeat Offenses If a citizen continues to show disrespect, they will face permanent banishment from the Empire. All their property up to Y-level 120 will be permanently seized by the crown.
VII

Article 7 — District Zoning Laws

Magic Town Amethyst Rights Amethyst blocks, budding amethyst, and sculk materials are strictly reserved for the paths and aesthetics of Magic Town. No citizen may use these blocks for personal housing construction without a Royal Permit.
Beach City Customs To maintain the weapon-free zone in the Capital under Article 5, Beach City docks will serve as the primary trade port. All external merchants must unload cargo there under the supervision of a Royal Guard.
VIII

Article 8 — The Deep Dark Classification Act

The Forbidden Zone The deep dark biome discovered under the city is classified as a Royal Sanctuary. Citizens are strictly forbidden from entering, exploring, or mining within 500 blocks of the Capital’s underground coordinates.
The Warden Protocol If a citizen accidentally breaches the Y-50 ceiling and activates a Sculk Shrieker, they are legally liable for any server panic caused. They must pay a $10,000 fine for endangering the crown.
