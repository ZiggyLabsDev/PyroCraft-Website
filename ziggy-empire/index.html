Preamble

By royal decree, the following articles establish the laws, rights, duties, and regulations governing all citizens and visitors of the glorious Ziggy Empire. This Constitution serves as the founding charter of the Realm and establishes the authority of the Crown over imperial affairs.

I

Article I — Citizenship

The Process

Citizenship is gained through the following two-step process:

  • Sign the physical book included in your free starter kit shulker.
  • Personally hand the signed book to the great, fair, and hard-working King, TheGreatZiggy.
II

Article II — Housing & Property Rights

Imperial Housing

Citizens have the right to occupy houses built within the Ziggy Empire, provided they keep up with their monthly payments under the applicable housing agreement.

Construction Restrictions

All imperial housing and districts are constructed exclusively by the King. No citizen may build or structurally modify any house or building within the Empire without direct Royal Approval.

External Property

Citizens may own private land outside the Empire, provided that each registered property occupies fewer than 30 chunks. Unless otherwise approved by the Crown, this means a maximum of 29 chunks per property.

For this Constitution, one chunk refers to a standard Minecraft chunk measuring 16 by 16 blocks horizontally.

Land Registration

Any land purchased outside the Empire must be registered in the official imperial database. A citizen discovered to own unregistered land may be subject to permanent expulsion from the Empire following review by the Crown.

Citizens are responsible for ensuring their registrations remain accurate and up to date.

Royal Exemption

King TheGreatZiggy is exempt from all restrictions imposed by this Article. The King retains sovereign authority to own land without limit, build anywhere at will, and operate without the database registration requirements imposed on citizens.

III

Article III — Law & Order

Prohibited Actions

Griefing, stealing, logout traps, and general trapping are strictly forbidden inside the Ziggy Empire. Additional combat restrictions are established under Amendment I of this Constitution.

Standard Criminal Fines

A member found responsible for a violation of imperial law shall pay the following amounts:

  • $5,000 in compensation to the victim.
  • $1,000 to the Imperial Treasury.

These amounts are denominated in the server's established currency or equivalent value as determined by the Crown.

The Royal Multiplier

When an offense covered by the preceding fine provisions is committed against the King or a member of royal status, both the victim compensation and the Imperial fee are multiplied by five.

  • $25,000 in compensation to the victim.
  • $5,000 to the Imperial Treasury.

Refusal or Inability to Pay

If a citizen refuses or is unable to pay an imposed fine within 48 hours of notification, the Crown may seize property belonging to that citizen within the Empire to recover the outstanding amount.

The offender must also surrender one Ender Pearl to the Empire. If the offender does not own one, the Empire shall provide a pearl for the purpose of satisfying this symbolic penalty.

The Crown shall determine the scope of any seizure proportionately to the unpaid obligation.

IV

Article IV — Zoning & Infrastructure

Vertical Limits

No building or modification inside the Empire may extend above Y-level 120 or excavate below Y-level 50 without a Royal Permit.

Unless otherwise specified in a permit, the limits apply to the highest and lowest blocks of the construction or excavation, respectively.

Basements & Tunneling

Citizens may dig basements directly beneath their designated plots, subject to the vertical limits established by this Article.

Tunneling into a neighbor's plot or beneath public roads without permission is classified as trespassing and may be subject to penalties under Article III.

V

Article V — Foreign Policy & National Security

Border Control

Non-citizens are welcome to visit the Capital but must deposit all weapons in the designated border storage chest before entering the weapon-free zone.

An ordinary Ender Chest is player-specific and must not be treated as shared confiscation storage. Any physical deposit required by this provision must use the designated chest controlled for this purpose.

State of War

Upon a formal declaration of war by the King, all able-bodied citizens must defend the Empire or supply vital resources, including food, armor, and potions, to the Royal Military.

Foreign Alliances

No citizen may form an independent alliance or treaty with outside factions without the express written permission of King TheGreatZiggy.

VI

Article VI — Lèse-Majesté (Disrespect of the King)

The Law

Deliberate impersonation of the King, treason against the Empire, credible threats against the Crown, and serious acts of deliberate disrespect toward King TheGreatZiggy are prohibited within imperial borders and official imperial communication channels.

The Crown shall determine whether reported conduct constitutes a violation. Ordinary disagreement or good-faith criticism shall not, by itself, establish impersonation or treason.

First Offense

For a first offense under this Article, the offender must surrender one Ender Pearl to the Royal Treasury as a token of submission and pay a $10,000 Imperial fine.

Repeat Offenses

Repeated violations may result in permanent banishment from the Empire.

Upon such banishment, the Crown may seize the offender's property within the Empire, including property extending up to Y-level 120, subject to the Crown's determination of the property covered by the seizure.

VII

Article VII — District Zoning Laws

Magic Town Amethyst Rights

Amethyst blocks, budding amethyst, and sculk materials are reserved for the paths and aesthetics of Magic Town.

No citizen may use these materials for personal housing construction within the designated district without a Royal Permit.

This restriction includes unauthorized removal or destruction of protected budding amethyst formations.

Beach City Customs

To maintain the weapon-free zone in the Capital under Article V, Beach City docks shall serve as the primary trade port.

All external merchants must unload their cargo there under the supervision of a Royal Guard, unless the Crown authorizes an alternative arrangement.

VIII

Article VIII — The Deep Dark Classification Act

The Forbidden Zone

The Deep Dark biome discovered beneath the city is classified as a Royal Sanctuary.

Citizens are forbidden from entering, exploring, or mining within a horizontal radius of 500 blocks from the designated Capital reference coordinates in the underground restricted area.

The official reference coordinates must be designated by the Crown. Entry into the restricted area requires a Royal Permit.

The Warden Protocol

Any citizen who breaches the Y-50 excavation limit without authorization and activates a Sculk Shrieker is responsible for the resulting disruption, subject to the circumstances of the incident.

An unauthorized breach that activates a Sculk Shrieker and endangers the Crown shall incur a $10,000 fine.

IX

Article IX — Imperial Amendments & Clarifications

The following amendments supplement the preceding Articles and shall be interpreted together with the rest of this Constitution.

A-I

Amendment I — Prohibition of Fighting

Prohibition of Combat

Fighting within the borders of the Ziggy Empire is strictly prohibited. All citizens, visitors, and other individuals within the Empire must refrain from engaging in combat against one another.

This prohibition applies regardless of whether one or more parties have player-versus-player combat disabled.

Violations & Penalties

Any individual who initiates or participates in prohibited fighting may face penalties at the discretion of the Crown, in accordance with Article III and the circumstances of the incident.

Self-defense may be considered when determining responsibility. Retaliation after the immediate threat has ended does not automatically qualify as self-defense.

Any authorized combat exception must be explicitly approved by the Crown.

A-II

Amendment II — Public Storage Rights

Designation of Public Storage

The public storage room located directly across the street from the meat shop is designated as a public-use facility for all citizens and authorized visitors of the Ziggy Empire.

Shared Access

All authorized users may take items from the public storage room and contribute items at their own discretion.

No individual may claim exclusive ownership of the facility's shared contents or prohibit others from using the room while it continues to serve its designated public purpose.

Preservation of the Shared Resource

Individuals must not remove items for the purpose of denying reasonable access to other users. The Crown may establish reasonable usage limits if necessary to preserve the facility's shared purpose.

The public storage room shall remain a shared resource maintained for the benefit and convenience of the Empire and its community.

A-III

Amendment III — Road Protection & Infrastructure Rights

Protection of Roads

All roads, highways, bridges, paths, and other transportation infrastructure constructed by King TheGreatZiggy are protected under imperial law, regardless of whether they are located within the Ziggy Empire, on claimed land, or on unclaimed land.

Prohibition of Road Interference

No individual may destroy, grief, obstruct, alter, or deliberately damage any protected road without the express permission of King TheGreatZiggy.

This prohibition applies even when a protected road crosses land that is unclaimed, unowned, or outside the official borders of the Ziggy Empire.

Public Access

Unless otherwise designated by the King, roads constructed by King TheGreatZiggy shall remain open for public travel.

Individuals may use these roads for transportation but may not claim ownership of, block, dismantle, or deliberately interfere with them without authorization.

Penalties for Violations

Any individual found responsible for deliberately damaging or obstructing a protected road shall repair the damage or compensate the King for the materials and labor reasonably required to restore it.

Additional fines or punishments may be imposed in accordance with Article III.
